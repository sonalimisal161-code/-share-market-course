ABOUT THIS COURSE
Welcome to Share Market Mastery!
This course is designed to help beginners understand the Indian stock market, its structure, investment methods, trading concepts, financial analysis, chart reading, and risk management.
The course follows a structured learning approach, starting with basic concepts and gradually introducing more advanced topics.
Course Objectives
By the end of this course, learners should be able to:
Understand how the stock market works.
Identify major Indian stock exchanges.
Differentiate between investing and trading.
Understand fundamental and technical analysis.
Read basic stock market charts.
Recognize common candlestick patterns.
Understand financial risk management.
Identify reliable stock market research resources.
COURSE CURRICULUM
Introduction to the Share Market
Understanding the Indian Stock Market
Types of Trading and Investing
Fundamental Analysis
Technical Analysis and Chart Reading
Candlestick Patterns
Risk Management and Trading Psychology
Stock Market Tools, Regulations and Final Assessment
